## Who we are

**Al-Borhan Association for Quran & Sunnah Service** (جمعية البرهان لخدمة السنة والقرآن) is a Saudi non-profit founded in December 2022 and licensed by the National Center for Non-Profit Sector Development (License No. 943), with technical supervision from King Abdulaziz City for Science and Technology (KACST).

We serve the Holy Quran, its sciences, and the Prophetic Sunnah through **creative digital initiatives, specialised programs, and quality partnerships** — designing and shipping the apps, platforms, and tools that make that knowledge easier to reach, learn, and live by.

## Get in touch

We welcome collaborators, researchers, developers, and volunteers who want to put technology at the service of the Quran and Sunnah.
